# Reading difficulty and theme review — 2026-10-06

## Findings and decisions

The previous jump from roughly 11–12 available letters at level 1 to all 29 letters at level 2 changed search difficulty much more than word length suggested. Short answers with double consonants, Norwegian vowels, ambiguous meanings or unfamiliar vocabulary were not necessarily easier.

Level 2 now offers eight distinct letters for three-letter answers and ten for longer answers. Every answer letter is included; buttons are displayed in keyboard order, not answer order, and can be used repeatedly. Children may explicitly open all letters without consuming a hint. This choice persists with the active puzzle; reducing letters still works. Levels 3–4 use the full keyboard. Each level-2 word has a short semantic clue that does not spell out the answer. It is baseline support and does not count as a requested hint.

HØY (less familiar vocabulary and a diphthong), ÅRE (less familiar tool and multiple meanings), and MOSE (less familiar vocabulary) move to level 3. Familiar double-consonant words remain at level 2 with letter and meaning support. This is a design judgment, not a validated developmental scale; observe children before tuning again. Unlock thresholds remain unchanged.

Primary themes follow the meaning being taught, rather than every location where an object might occur. EGG is taught through the hen, so its primary theme is the farm; it can also be food, but home was an unhelpfully broad clue. Food, clothes, pets, and play/reading now have separate themes. Home contains building parts, furnishings and household objects. On-the-mountain becomes On-a-trip, fitting maps, packs and tents; snow moves to weather. Plant and food themes are separate. Ambiguous words such as ROM and VANN get specific meaning clues. Existing word IDs, discoveries, history and profile storage remain compatible.

## Follow-up: level 3 bridge — 2026-10-06

Keep level 2's limited keyboard and word-specific meaning support through level 3. The full keyboard is now automatic only at level 4 (still optional earlier). Every level-3 word has 5–6 letters, ten available letter choices and a semantic clue. Familiar longer words BANAN, KANIN, GULROT and SKOLE move into level 3. SNØ, ÅRE and HØY use level 2's existing semantic clues and limited keyboard instead of occupying the longer-word group. Existing level-2 words and clues are unchanged. Updated counts: 21 / 72 / 16 / 11. This supersedes the earlier placement of HØY and ÅRE and the earlier full-keyboard threshold. Word IDs and saved progress remain compatible.

Four-letter words keep level-2 support. Longer familiar words BJØRN, FJELL, RAKETT, HJERTE, SYKKEL, NØKKEL and SITRON also join the supported level-3 group, each with a concrete meaning clue. The earlier recommendation to postpone MOSE is superseded by retaining concrete meaning support for all four-letter answers.
